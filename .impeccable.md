# Design Context — Waterfall

## Target audience
Technical decision-makers at US/European companies — CTOs, VPs of Engineering, founders,
heads of product — who are evaluating an outsourced engineering partner. They are skeptical,
have seen a hundred agency sites, and are scanning for signal: real clients, real shipped work,
real engineering depth. Secondary audience: senior engineers considering joining.

## Use cases
1. Assess credibility in under 30 seconds (who has Waterfall worked with?)
2. Inspect concrete project work and outcomes
3. Discover Waterfall's own products (Zenda) — proof the team ships, not just staffs
4. Start a conversation

## Brand personality / tone
Precise, engineered, quietly confident. An engineering ledger, not a sales brochure.
Never hypey, never "we're passionate about innovation." Restraint reads as competence.
The name **Waterfall** is the visual thesis: things descend, cascade, and flow — expressed
through vertical rhythm and falling motion, never through literal water imagery.

## Aesthetic direction — "Engineering ledger meets editorial"
- **Monochrome, blue-tinted neutrals.** Never pure black or pure white.
- **One accent, used like punctuation.** A single signal blue, reserved for: the live status dot,
  one column in the hero cascade, the scroll-progress rail, focus rings, and one link underline
  per section. If blue appears more than a few times per viewport, it is wrong.
- **Editorial typography.** Instrument Sans (grotesque) for everything, with Instrument Serif
  *italic* used for exactly one emphasized word per major headline. Geist Mono only for genuine
  metadata (indices, years, counts) — never as "technical vibes" decoration.
- **Hairlines over cards.** Structure comes from 1px rules and asymmetric column spans on a
  12-column grid, not from boxes with shadows.
- **Signature element:** the hero cascade — a field of thin vertical columns with light segments
  falling at varied speeds. Exactly one column is blue.

## Constraints
Next.js 16 (App Router) · React 19 · Tailwind v4 · Motion · Lenis smooth scroll.
Must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Subdomain routing (`zenda.<domain>`) via middleware.
